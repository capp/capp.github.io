Digest Player

Long videos, in less time.

Play three seconds at normal speed, then move the next three seconds along at 3×. Repeating this, you can follow the story while getting through the video at a steady pace. It suits lectures, seminars, explainer videos and recorded shows: videos you don't have time to watch in full but still want to understand. For iPhone and iPad.

Features

  • Digest playback: choose the normal seconds, the fast-forward speed (2–10×) and its seconds by turning wheels. You can also pick “Skip” to jump ahead instead of fast-forwarding
  • Thumbnail seek bar: frames from the whole video line up, and dragging shows the frame under your finger in a bubble, so you can jump to any scene
  • One-handed controls: a floating control panel at the side of the screen (skip back, hold for normal speed or fast forward, skip forward), swipes to seek and change the volume, double-tap to pause, and a diagonal swipe down to close
  • Loop playback: start again from the beginning when the video ends
  • Continue watching and history: pick up right where you left off. History keeps how far you watched, with thumbnails
  • Built-in guide: tap “?” at the top right while watching to see the swipes and taps at any time
  • Network videos (Premium): play videos straight from NAS or computer shares (SMB) and media servers (DLNA), with favorite folders
  • Full-screen thumbnails (Premium): frames from the whole video in many rows across the screen, for a closer look at the flow
  • Two sets of starting values (Premium): keep one set for lectures and one for movies, and switch by swiping the wheels
