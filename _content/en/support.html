FAQ

What is digest playback?
It alternates a stretch of normal playback with a stretch of fast-forward. Change the seconds and speed by turning the wheels in the main control panel at the bottom of the screen up or down.
How do I use the controls?
Open a video and tap “?” at the top right to see the swipes and taps.
What does “Skip” do?
If you choose “Skip” as the digest speed, the player jumps ahead by the forward seconds instead of fast-forwarding.
Some videos won't open
Digest Player supports the formats iPhone and iPad can play (MP4, MOV, M4V and so on). Some formats, such as MKV, can't be played.
I can't connect to my NAS or shared folder
Check the user name and password. To connect without a password, use “Connect as Guest” in the sign-in screen (the server must allow guest access). If no shares appear as a guest, tap “Connect with User Name” and sign in with a user name and password.
A partly watched video won't open from my DLNA server
Universal Media Server up to 15.8 converts partly watched videos before sending them. Update to 15.9.0 or later, or turn off the server's resume feature.
Thumbnails from my DLNA server have a circular arrow on them
Universal Media Server adds this mark to partly watched and fully played videos. In the server's settings, set “Fully played action” to “Do nothing”, then clear the server's cache. To continue a video, open it from Continue watching or History in the app (opening it from the folder list starts from the beginning).
I want to use my purchase on another device
On a device signed in with the same Apple Account, open Premium at the top of Settings and tap “Restore Purchases.”
How do I remove ads?
Ads go away with Premium. Start from “Remove ads” next to an ad, or from Premium at the top of Settings.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel at any time from the device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
How do I clear the history?
In History, swipe a row left to remove it, or use the button at the top right to clear everything.
